# New Jersey home-care program limits for 2026 (curated summary)

Curated by the CareOneX team from the NJ Division of Aging Services "Programs Side-by-Side (2026)"
table (published March 2026, https://www.nj.gov/humanservices/doas/documents/DoAS%20Programs%20Side-by-Side%20(2026).pdf)
and the NJ FamilyCare "MLTSS Application Guidance" (April 2026). Figures below apply to 2026 unless a
row says otherwise; the state updates them each January and March. A county agency decides eligibility;
these are the published limits, not a decision.

## MLTSS (NJ FamilyCare / Medicaid Managed Long Term Services and Supports) 2026

- Who: age 21 or older, needs nursing-home level of care (hands-on help with 3 or more daily activities, or
  cognitive deficits needing supervision with 3 or more).
- Income limit 2026: $2,982 per month for a single applicant. Income above that requires a Qualified
  Income Trust (QIT). Couples both applying: $5,964 combined.
- Resource (asset) limit 2026: $2,000 for a single applicant ($3,000 couple both applying). The home you
  live in and one vehicle do not count. A non-applicant spouse may keep up to $162,660 in 2026.
- Look-back: 5 years of financial records; transfers for less than fair value cause a penalty period.
- Simplified application (self-attestation of no transfers) possible if income is $1,330 per month or less in 2026.
- Cost share: none for in-home services; patient-pay liability may apply in assisted living or a nursing facility.
- Apply: online at the NJ FamilyCare application site or through the County Social Service Agency (CSSA);
  clinical screening through the Area Agency on Aging / ADRC at 1-877-222-3737.

## JACC (Jersey Assistance for Community Caregiving) 2026

- Who: New Jersey resident age 60 or older, living at home (not a facility), nursing-home level of care,
  not enrolled in a Medicaid program.
- Countable income limit 2026: $4,855 per month for an individual; $6,582 per month for a couple (365% of the federal poverty level).
- Resource (asset) limit 2026: $40,000 individual; $60,000 couple.
- Cost share: sliding scale based on income.
- Service cap: up to $1,090 per month of services.
- Services: care management, respite, home modifications, medical equipment and supplies, chore, personal
  emergency response system, attendant care, home-delivered meals, social adult day care, home-based
  supportive care, adult day health, transportation.
- Apply: county Area Agency on Aging / ADRC, 1-877-222-3737. State funded; no federal Medicaid match.

## Statewide Respite Care Program (SRCP) 2026

- Who: care recipient age 18 or older with a chronic disability, living in the community with an unpaid
  caregiver who needs a break. Not available to people enrolled in MLTSS.
- Care recipient income limit 2026: $2,982 per month individual; $5,964 per month couple.
- Resource limit 2026: $40,000 individual; $60,000 couple.
- Cost share: sliding scale. Services vary with the caregiver's assessed need and budget availability.
- Services: companion, homemaker or home health aide, private duty nursing, adult day health, social adult
  day care, adult family care, short inpatient stays (assisted living, nursing home), campership, caregiver-directed option.
- Apply: county SRCP sponsor agency via the ADRC, 1-877-222-3737.

## Alzheimer's Adult Day Services Program (AADSP)

- Who: person with Alzheimer's disease or a related dementia, living in the community with an unpaid caregiver.
- Gross income limit (2025 figure still shown in the 2026 table): $50,256 per year individual; $58,632 per year couple.
- Resource limit: $40,000 individual; $60,000 couple.
- Cost share: sliding scale. Up to 5 days per week of adult day services.

## Congregate Housing Services Program (CHSP)

- Who: residents of participating subsidized senior/disabled housing sites who request services and are
  assessed as needing them. Financial eligibility follows the housing site's HUD income rules.
- Services: congregate meals, housekeeping, laundry, shopping, help with bathing, grooming and dressing.
- Cost share: sliding scale.

## Older Americans Act (OAA) services

- Who: age 60 or older; some caregiver services at any age and grandparents 55 or older. No means test;
  priority to greatest economic and social need.
- Services: information and assistance, options counseling, care management, transportation, certified
  home health aide, housekeeping, home-delivered and congregate meals, legal assistance, adult day care.
- Cost: no fee; voluntary contributions invited. Availability differs by county and budget.

## Related 2026 benefit programs (NJSave application, www.aging.nj.gov or 1-800-792-9745)

- PAAD (prescription help): annual income under $54,943 individual or $62,390 couple; no resource limit;
  $5 generic / $7 brand copays. Age 65+, or 18 to 64 on Social Security Disability.
- Senior Gold: annual income $54,943 to $64,943 individual or $62,390 to $72,390 couple.
- Medicare Savings Programs: QMB annual income up to $15,960 individual / $21,640 couple; SLMB and QI up to
  $21,546 / $29,214; resources up to $9,950 / $14,910 for all three. QMB pays Part A and B premiums,
  deductibles and copays; SLMB and QI pay the Part B premium ($202.90 per month in 2026).
- Lifeline utility assistance: $225 per year; same income limits as PAAD.
- Hearing Aid Assistance (HAAAD): $500 reimbursement per device, up to $1,000 for two; same income limits as PAAD.
