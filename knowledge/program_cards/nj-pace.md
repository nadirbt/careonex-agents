## Summary

PACE bundles Medicare and Medicaid coverage into one program run by an
interdisciplinary care team, combining a few center visits a week with
in-home services, aimed at people who need nursing-home-level care but can
still live safely in the community. As of early 2026, PACE expanded to cover
all 21 NJ counties (previously only available in some counties) — this is
new enough that a caller may not know it now applies where they live.

## Who qualifies

- Age 55 or older.
- Meets NJ's clinical criteria for nursing-home-level care.
- Able to live safely in the community at the time of enrollment, with
  PACE's help.
- Lives within a specific PACE organization's service area (service areas
  are defined by zip code and vary by center — always confirm the specific
  center covering the caller's zip, do not assume by county alone).

## What it pays for

Everything Medicare and Medicaid would otherwise cover, without the usual
limitations of either program separately, plus anything else the
interdisciplinary team decides is necessary to keep the person living at
home — this can include primary and specialty care, medications, home care,
transportation to the center, and more. A participant can leave PACE at any
time and will be helped transitioning back to their prior coverage.

## How to apply

Contact the PACE organization serving the caller's area directly, or the NJ
Division of Aging Services. As of the most recent source found, eight PACE
organizations operate across the state's 21 counties, each with a defined
zip-code service area — do not promise a specific center without checking
its zip list first.

## Key numbers

- Minimum age: 55.
- Statewide as of Jan/Feb 2026: expanded to all 21 counties (previously
  partial coverage) — worth mentioning proactively since it's recent.

## What to say on the call

"There's a program called PACE that combines Medicare and Medicaid coverage
with a care team and in-home visits — it just expanded to cover the whole
state this year. It depends on your exact zip code which center covers you,
so let me check that before I say more."
