## Summary

Two different VA benefits, often confused. **Aid & Attendance / Housebound**
is a monthly cash add-on to a VA pension the veteran or survivor can spend on
any care they choose. **Homemaker & Home Health Aide Care** is a VA-arranged
in-home aide service, separate from the cash benefit. A family may qualify
for one, both, or neither — ask about VA pension status before assuming.

## Who qualifies — Aid & Attendance / Housebound

Must already receive a VA pension, plus at least one of:

- **Aid & Attendance:** needs another person's help with daily activities
  (bathing, feeding, dressing); OR must stay in bed / spend most of the day
  in bed due to illness; OR is a nursing home patient due to loss of mental
  or physical ability; OR has significantly limited eyesight (5/200 or less
  even with correction, or 5-degree visual field).
- **Housebound:** spends most time at home due to a permanent disability.
- Cannot receive both Aid & Attendance and Housebound at the same time.

## Who qualifies — Homemaker & Home Health Aide Care

- Must be an enrolled VA health care beneficiary.
- Eligible for community care and meets clinical criteria (typically: needs
  help with 2+ activities of daily living, or significant cognitive
  impairment, or hospice-related personal care need, or a documented
  clinical justification from the care team).
- Service availability varies by VA facility/location — always check local
  availability, don't assume it's offered everywhere.
- A copay may apply depending on service-connected disability status.

## What it pays for

- **Aid & Attendance / Housebound:** a monthly dollar amount added to the
  veteran's or survivor's pension — spendable on any care arrangement, not
  restricted to VA-contracted providers.
- **Homemaker & Home Health Aide:** a trained aide (supervised by an RN) who
  helps with daily activities and light housekeeping in the home; can serve
  as an alternative to nursing home care or give the family caregiver
  respite.

## How to apply

- **Aid & Attendance / Housebound:** apply online, by mail (VA Form
  21-2680, with a medical examiner section completed), or in person at a VA
  regional office. If in a nursing home, also submit VA Form 21-0779.
  Mail to: VA Pension Intake Center, PO Box 5365, Janesville, WI
  53547-5365. Processing time varies; claims are worked in order received.
- **Homemaker & Home Health Aide:** talk to a VA social worker at the
  veteran's VA facility to arrange a needs assessment.

## Key numbers

- Specific 2026 pension/Aid & Attendance dollar amounts were not confirmed
  in the sources checked for this card — **do not quote a dollar figure
  until verified against the current VA pension rate table** (published
  annually by VA).

## What to say on the call

"If your father is a veteran already getting a VA pension, there's an
add-on called Aid and Attendance that pays extra each month toward any care
he needs. There's also a separate VA program that can send a trained aide to
the home. I'd want to check his current VA enrollment before saying which
one applies."
