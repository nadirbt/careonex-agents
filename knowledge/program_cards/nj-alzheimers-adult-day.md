## Summary

Subsidizes adult day care specifically for people with Alzheimer's disease
or a related dementia, covering up to five days a week. This is narrower
than JACC or Statewide Respite — it requires a physician-documented dementia
diagnosis and only applies at one of a limited set of participating centers.

## Who qualifies

- Physician-documented diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease or a related
  dementia.
- 18 or older, NJ resident, living in the community.
- Receives daily basic care/supervision from an uncompensated caregiver
  18+.
- Meets income/asset guidelines. 2025 figures found: single person max
  $50,256/year income, $40,000 liquid assets; married max $58,632/year
  combined income, $60,000 combined assets. **Confirm current year's figures
  before quoting** — these were the most recent found and may have updated.
- **Not** currently in a Medicaid program, JACC, Statewide Respite, or
  Congregate Housing Services Program (switching from JACC or Statewide
  Respite into this program is allowed).

## What it pays for

Subsidy covering roughly 20%–100% of the cost of attending adult day care,
up to 5 days/week, at one of the program's contracted centers (21 centers
across 14 counties as of the most recent source found — coverage is
uneven across counties, so always check with the specific center).
Additional days may be covered by private pay or other programs.

## How to apply

Applications are completed directly with the participating adult day center,
not through a separate state application. Call the ADRC (**1-877-222-3737**)
to find the nearest participating center.

## Key numbers

- Subsidy range: 20%–100% of adult day care cost.
- Up to 5 subsidized days/week.
- Income limits (2025, needs re-verification): ~$50,256/yr individual,
  ~$58,632/yr couple; assets $40,000/$60,000.

## What to say on the call

"If your parent has an Alzheimer's or dementia diagnosis, there's a program
that can subsidize adult day care specifically — sometimes covering the
full cost. It only works through certain participating centers, so I'd want
to check which one is closest to you."
