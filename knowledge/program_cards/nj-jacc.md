## Summary

JACC is a **state-funded, non-Medicaid** program for New Jersey residents 60
and older who need nursing-home-level care but want to stay at home, and who
do not qualify financially for Medicaid MLTSS. It is often the right answer
for a family that has "too much" income or savings for Medicaid but still
needs help paying for care.

## Who qualifies

- NJ resident, age 60 or older, US citizen or qualified alien.
- Requires nursing facility level of care (generally, needs help with a
  minimum of three activities of daily living).
- **Not** enrolled in a Medicaid program (NJ FamilyCare / MLTSS) — JACC is
  specifically for people who don't qualify for or aren't using those.
- Not currently in the Alzheimer's Adult Day Services Program or Statewide
  Respite Care Program (can switch between these programs, just not use two
  at once).
- Countable resources at or below **$40,000 for an individual, $60,000 for a
  couple** (home equity is excluded).
- Financial eligibility is capped around 365% of the Federal Poverty Level
  for income — treat any specific monthly income figure as approximate and
  confirm with the local ADRC, since sources vary ($4,855/mo individual in
  one secondary source, $2,982/mo elsewhere for a related program — don't
  guess on the call).
- Lives in the community (not in an assisted living facility, nursing home,
  or residential care facility).
- Has no alternate way to get the needed support.

## What it pays for

Care management plus a menu of in-home and community services tailored to a
plan of care: home health aide, respite care, home modification, special
medical equipment/supplies, chore service, personal emergency response
system, home-delivered meals, adult day care, transportation. Participants
can sometimes hire their own caregivers, including certain family, friends,
or neighbors.

Services are limited to roughly **$1,090–$1,156 per month** depending on
source and year (figures found ranged from $1,090 to $1,156; confirm current
cap before quoting), plus care management, with a sliding-scale co-pay based
on income.

## How to apply

Contact the county Aging and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) /
Area Agency on Aging. Statewide number: **1-877-222-3737**. Applications
route through the county's "NJ EASE" single-entry point.

## Key numbers

- Asset limit: $40,000 individual / $60,000 couple (home excluded).
- Monthly service cap: approximately $1,090–$1,156 — **verify current figure
  before quoting a caller**, do not average the two.
- Statewide ADRC intake line: 1-877-222-3737.

## What to say on the call

"If Medicaid isn't the right fit because of income or savings, there's a
state program called JACC that helps pay for home care for people 60 and
up, up to a set monthly amount. I can get you connected to your county's
aging office to check the exact numbers and start an application."
