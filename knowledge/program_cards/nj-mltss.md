## Summary

MLTSS is New Jersey's Medicaid program that pays for long-term care —
including help at home — for people who need a nursing-home level of care
but qualify financially and clinically for Medicaid. Since 2014, NJ delivers
almost all Medicaid long-term care through managed care (an HMO-style plan),
not by billing the state directly.

## Who qualifies

Two separate eligibility tracks must both be met:

- **Clinical eligibility** — determined by the Division of Aging Services.
  Generally means needing help with a minimum of three activities of daily
  living (ADLs): bathing, toileting, dressing, transfers, locomotion.
- **Financial eligibility** — determined by the local County Social Service
  Agency (CSSA), using the "Aged, Blind, Disabled" (ABD) application. Both
  income and countable assets must be within program limits.
  - An applicant who is clinically eligible for MLTSS qualifies at a higher
    income limit than standard Medicaid.
  - If gross monthly income exceeds the limit, a Qualified Income Trust
    (QIT) can be established with the excess to still qualify. This is
    common and not a red flag — say so if a caller sounds anxious about it.

Both evaluations run concurrently and are coordinated between the two
agencies — a family does not need to complete one before starting the other.

## What it pays for

Comprehensive long-term care in the setting of the person's choice:
assisted living, group home, nursing home, or the person's own home. In-home
services can include personal care assistance, home health aide visits, and
care management, coordinated by the person's Medicaid managed care
organization (MCO).

## How to apply

1. Contact the County Social Service Agency (CSSA) in the county where the
   applicant lives to start the ABD financial application. Can apply online
   or by paper.
2. In parallel, request a clinical (functional) assessment for nursing
   facility level of care. If already enrolled in NJ FamilyCare, request this
   through the managed care organization; otherwise contact the local Aging
   and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) at **1-877-222-3737** or the
   CSSA.
3. Free legal help with the application is available by county; the DMAHS
   site links a directory.

## Key numbers

- Figures such as the enhanced MLTSS income limit and asset limit change
  annually and are not restated here with confidence — a search result
  citing a 2022 figure ($2,523/mo income, $2,000 assets) is too old to
  repeat on a call. **Do not quote a specific dollar limit until this card
  is verified against the current DMAHS ABD-MLTSS brochure
  (`NJFC-ABD-MLTSS-BRO-E-2026_Web.pdf`) or the applicant's managed care
  organization.**
- Qualified Income Trusts (QITs) exist specifically to let someone with
  income just above the limit still qualify — mention this as an option
  before assuming someone is ineligible.

## What to say on the call

"Medicaid can pay for home care through a program called MLTSS, but it takes
two steps — a financial application through your county and a separate
nursing-care-level assessment. I can help start both, or connect you with
someone who tracks the current income limits exactly."
